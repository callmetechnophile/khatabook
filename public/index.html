01 / The Everyday Reality

Pages fade. Memories get tested.
Trust shouldn't.

In the morning rush of a neighborhood grocery store, five customers ask for ration at once. Hands are dusted with flour; time is short.

Traditional paper notebooks (bahi-khata) have served India for centuries, but daily store life takes its toll. Incomplete entries, ink smeared by spilled tea, loose slips of paper, and calculations done in haste lead to quiet doubts and awkward month-end disputes.

Small retailers do not lose money because they lack customers. They lose money because small credit slips slip through the cracks unremembered.

The Fragility of Paper

Physical ledgers tear, fade, and cannot be searched or backed up. A single lost book erases months of credit records.

The Awkward Misunderstanding

When handwriting is ambiguous or an entry is overlooked, asking a loyal neighbor to pay twice tests community relationships.

The Typing Dilemma

Modern accounting apps force store owners to navigate ten nested menus and type names on small phone screens while customers wait.

02 / The Turning Point

You shouldn't have to choose
between typing and serving.

A digital ledger for everyday Indian commerce must feel as effortless as speaking across the counter, as immediate as cash, and as private as a personal vault.

01

Spoken, Not Typed

Simply hold the button and speak naturally in Hindi, Kannada, Tamil, or English. Speak full transactions, payments, or balance inquiries in one fluid sentence.

02

100% Offline-First

Zero dependency on spotty 2G mobile data or cloud roundtrips. Every credit and debit entry is committed instantly to encrypted on-device storage.

03

Private by Default

Protected with AES-256 local database encryption. Balances are masked from wandering eyes, and financial data is never mined for behavioral advertising.
